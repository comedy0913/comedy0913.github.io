# 👋 About Me

I am a first-year Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Technology at [Xi'an Jiaotong University](https://www.xjtu.edu.cn/) (XJTU), jointly supervised by [Prof. Wei Ke](https://gr.xjtu.edu.cn/en/web/wei.ke) (XJTU) and [Prof. Tong Zhang](https://people.ucas.ac.cn/~tongzhang) (University of Chinese Academy of Sciences). I received my B.Eng. degree from XJTU in 2024 and studied as a Master's student there from 2024 to 2026.

My research interests lie in 3D vision and world models. I am always open to collaboration — feel free to drop me an email!
